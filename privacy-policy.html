Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Wiz Fuel Density is an independent utility app intended to help fuel-retail users calculate fuel density corrected to 15°C.

Information the app handles

Every calculation works without an internet connection. Your density readings, with their date and time, and your light or dark theme choice are saved on your device, inside the app. The app has no file export. A reading leaves the app only when you copy it or share it yourself, through the share sheet or app that you choose.

Collection and sharing

The app does not require you to create an account. Your density readings and history are not sent to the developer, and the developer does not receive or sell them.

Your device, Android, Google Play, or any app you choose to share a reading with may process information under their own privacy policies.

Internet permission and ads

The app has the Internet permission. This version uses it only to let Google Play check for and deliver app updates and to show Google Play's rating card. This version does not show ads and does not send your readings, locations or any identifier to us or to an advertising service. A future version may show ads from Google AdMob; this policy will be updated before that happens.

The calculator page inside the app is blocked from the network: it cannot load or send anything over the internet.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your saved readings and theme choice in your device backup, so they come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account settings; the developer cannot see it.

Data retention and deletion

Saved history remains on your device until you delete it within the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. A copy may also stay in your Android device backup until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Children's privacy

Wiz Fuel Density is a general utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Security

Because history is stored locally, protect access to your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, email blazewizofficial@gmail.com.
