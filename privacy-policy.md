Privacy Policy — Wiz Fuel Density
Effective date: 29 October 2026

Wiz Fuel Density is an independent utility app intended to help fuel-retail users calculate fuel density corrected to 15°C.
Information the app handles
Wiz Fuel Density is designed to work offline. Density readings and their associated date and time may be saved in the app's history on your device. The app may allow you to export saved history as a CSV file. Exported files are stored or shared only through actions you initiate on your device.
Collection and sharing
The app does not require you to create an account. The app's core functionality is designed to operate without sending your density readings or history to the developer. The developer does not receive or sell this locally stored history.

Your device, Android, Google Play, or any file-sharing service you choose may process information under their own privacy policies.
Permissions
The app does not need an account or a server connection for its core density-calculation and history features. Android may display system prompts when you choose to export or share a file.
Data retention and deletion
Saved history remains on your device until you delete it within the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Keep your own copy of any CSV export you need to retain.
Children's privacy
Wiz Fuel Density is a general utility and is not designed specifically for children.
Security
Because history is stored locally, protect access to your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.
Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
Contact
For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details provided on the Wiz Fuel Density Google Play listing.
